**Subject:** Request about the Data-Campaign pond water-quality data (M.Tech research, licence and further data)

Dear [name / Data team],

I am an M.Tech student at [university], working under [guide's name], on a research project titled "An aquatic model for water quality prediction and interpretation using explainable AI". We use the 17 continuous pond CSV files from your Data-Campaign (Eluru, 28 November 2025 to 30 January 2026) to forecast dissolved oxygen, pH and water temperature, and to explain the forecasts. Thank you for publishing this data; it is rare to find real pond sensor data with this detail.

We would be grateful for your help with five questions:

1. **Licence.** We could not find a licence file for the data. May we use it for academic research, and publish derived results (statistics, figures and trained-model results) in a thesis and a paper? Is there an attribution or citation you prefer? We do not redistribute the raw files.
2. **More data.** Do you hold other continuous-sensor campaigns, other seasons (for example warmer or monsoon months), other regions, or later periods in the same format? Our biggest limit is that we have about two months in one cool season. More seasons and farms would let us test whether the method generalises.
3. **Context data.** Do you have records of aerator on/off times, feeding, stocking density, pond size and depth, or farm identifiers (even anonymised) that could be shared? These explain much of the oxygen cycle and could improve forecasts.
4. **Data quality.** In the files, we found zero readings, duplicate timestamps with conflicting values, and flags such as DO below 0.1 mg/L. Could you tell us whether zero readings usually mean a sensor fault or a true reading, and whether a data dictionary exists?
5. **Possible pilot.** If our results hold up on unseen ponds, would you be open to a small shadow trial, in which forecasts are produced for selected ponds and compared with what actually happened, with no action taken on them? We would share all results with you.

We are following a strict protocol: held-out ponds and later days for testing, a sealed final test, and every result reported, including negative ones. We would be happy to share our write-up with you.

Thank you for your time.
