      Things to keep in mind

        Words we use

        What is dissolved oxygen (mg/L)?

        The amount of oxygen mixed in the water, measured in milligrams per litre. Fish and other pond life breathe it. It usually falls at night and rises in daylight.

        What is pH?

        A number that says how acidic or basic the water is. Many ponds sit between about 7 and 9. It also moves up and down through the day.

        What does "likely between" mean?

        It is a range. In our tests on ponds the computer had never seen, the real value fell inside a range like this about 9 times out of 10.

        What is "a plain guess"?

        Repeating the latest reading ("same as now") or the reading from the same time yesterday. We always compare the computer model with these, and we tell you when the plain guess is just as good.

        What is the "chance of very low oxygen"?

        The computer's estimate of how likely oxygen is to drop below 2 mg/L, a very low level, at some point in the next hours. It is experimental: it spots risky situations better than simple rules, but it also raises false alarms.

        Why are some reasons shown with a warning?

        For each answer we show the three biggest reasons the model gave. When those reasons changed from test to test, we say so and ask you to treat them as a rough guide.
